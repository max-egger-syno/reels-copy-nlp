# Copy cards: what goes viral within each creator

Built from within-creator comparisons: each creator's most viral reels (top 30% of lift over their own rolling median) against their least viral (bottom 30%). Words are kept only if they rank in the top 15 in at least 70% of 200 bootstrap refits. Examples are real Portuguese copy from the creator's own viral reels.


## AI  (@beprecht, @odanilogato)

Per-creator model picks the more viral reel in 68% of 31 held-out pairs.

**Words that ride with virality:** `um robô` (@odanilogato)

**Words that ride with flops:** `agente de` (@odanilogato)

**Devices that agree across this sector's creators:** higher lift with a follow CTA (mean rho +0.15); lower lift with opening perplexity (catchphrase-ness) (mean rho -0.13).
